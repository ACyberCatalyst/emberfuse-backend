EmberFuse Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how EmberFuse handles information when you play the game. EmberFuse does not require you to create an account or provide an email address to play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings, display name, a randomly generated installation identifier, and local game progress on your device. This information supports saved games, settings, and leaderboard features.

Leaderboard information

If you use the leaderboard, EmberFuse sends your chosen display name, randomly generated installation identifier, and score to the EmberFuse leaderboard service hosted by Render. The service uses these details to reserve unique names, keep each installation's personal-best score, and rank players. Your display name and score appear publicly in the leaderboard; your installation identifier is not shown to other players. Choose a nickname and do not put private information in it.

The leaderboard information is retained while the entry remains active. To request that your leaderboard entry be removed, contact the developer using the support email shown on the EmberFuse Google Play listing and include your display name and score so the entry can be located.

Ads and connectivity checks

EmberFuse uses Google AdMob to request interstitial ads. Google may process device or advertising identifiers, network information such as IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Data handling may depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's services. Where required, the game uses Google's consent tools before initializing ads.

Online play requires a connection. The game contacts a Google connectivity-check endpoint to determine whether the internet is available; Google may receive network information such as your IP address as part of that request.

How information is shared

Your display name and score are shared publicly through the leaderboard. The game also uses Render to host the leaderboard service and Google services for connectivity checks and ads. Information is shared with these providers only as needed to provide those features and as described in their privacy information.

Security and retention

Game requests to the leaderboard use HTTPS. Local game data remains on your device unless you uninstall the game or clear its data. Leaderboard information remains on the service until it is removed following a request described above. No online account password is collected by EmberFuse.

Children

EmberFuse is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a public leaderboard name, contact the developer using the support email on the Google Play listing so the entry can be reviewed or removed.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-removal requests, contact the developer using the support email shown on the EmberFuse Google Play listing. The app name is EmberFuse.
