Privacy Policy for Our Games

Publisher: NehmehR

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how our games handle information when you play. Features and data practices may vary by game.

Information stored on your device

Our games may save settings, display names, randomly generated installation identifiers, and local game progress on your device. The information saved depends on the game and the features you use.

Leaderboard information

When one of our games offers a leaderboard and you use it, that game sends your chosen display name, randomly generated installation identifier, and score to its leaderboard service. Depending on the game, the service may be operated by us or hosted by a third-party provider such as Render. The service uses these details to reserve unique names, keep each installation's personal-best score, and rank players. Your display name and score appear publicly on that game's leaderboard; your installation identifier is not shown to other players. Choose a nickname and do not put private information in it.

Leaderboard information is retained while the entry remains active. To request removal, contact the developer using the support email shown on the relevant game's Google Play listing and include your display name and score so the entry can be located.

Ads and connectivity checks

Games that show ads use Google AdMob to request them. Google may process device or advertising identifiers, network information such as IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Data handling may depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's services. Where required, each game uses Google's consent tools before initializing ads.

Some of our games require an internet connection to play. When a game checks connectivity, it contacts a Google connectivity-check endpoint; Google may receive network information such as your IP address as part of that request.

How information is shared

When you use a leaderboard, your display name and score are shared publicly on that leaderboard. Games with leaderboard features may use Render to host the service; games with ads or connectivity checks may use Google services. Information is shared with these providers as needed for the features you use and as described in their privacy information.

If you choose to rate one of our games, its Google Play listing opens. Google Play handles any rating or review you submit under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive review text through our games.

Security and retention

Requests from our games to online leaderboards use HTTPS. Local data remains on your device unless you uninstall the relevant game or clear its data. Leaderboard information remains on the service until it is removed following a request described above. Our games do not collect online account passwords.

Children and families

Our games do not ask players to provide their age. Each game's Google Play target-audience declaration and applicable ad settings describe its intended audience. If a child has included personal information in a public leaderboard name, contact the developer using the support email on that game's Google Play listing and request removal.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-removal requests, contact the developer using the support email shown on the relevant game's Google Play listing. Include the game name and the display name or score associated with your request.
